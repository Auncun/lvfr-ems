Privacy policy

Service: LVFR EMS Operations portal.

This policy explains the information handled by this portal and why. The portal is for LVFR EMS roster and operational administration.

Information used

  • LVFR accounts use a member name and password. The account is associated with the roster member and follows that member's current callsign.
  • Passwords are transmitted over HTTPS. The server stores a salted, repeatedly hashed password value and keeps a temporary session token in your browser session.
  • Operational records can include roster details, rank and callsign changes, training and exam status, activity, notes, watch-command logs, and account audit events.

How information is used and stored

Information is used to authenticate accounts, administer LVFR access, maintain EMS roster and training records, and document operational activity. Account and operational records are stored in private Google Sheets controlled by LVFR and are available to authorized LVFR administrators according to their roles. Google Sheets stores the records; Cloudflare Pages delivers the website and proxies API requests.

The session token is held temporarily in your browser session and sent over HTTPS to authenticate requests. Passwords are not written to the spreadsheet; only their salt and derived hash are stored.

Sharing, retention, and choices

Information is used for LVFR operations and is not used by this portal for advertising. It is available to authorized LVFR personnel and the service providers needed to operate hosting and spreadsheets.

The application has no automatic deletion schedule. Records may remain in LVFR spreadsheets, including audit history, after an account is deactivated or marked removed. To ask about access, correction, or removal of account information, contact an LVFR Commander through your established LVFR contact channel.

You can end the browser session by signing out or closing the browser tab.
