LVFR EMS Operations

This portal supports authorized LVFR EMS personnel with roster administration, training and examination records, activity status, and watch-command logs. Access is limited to accounts linked to an LVFR roster entry and approved by an LVFR Commander.

Name and password access

Sign in with the member name shown on the LVFR roster and your password. Passwords are sent over HTTPS and stored as salted password hashes. The account follows the member's current callsign in the roster.

New account requests ask for the member name and a password. The name must match one member on the roster, and a Commander must approve the request before the account can access the portal.
