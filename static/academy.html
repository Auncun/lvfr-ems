Recruit

The recruit is placed in the first empty Callsign of the chosen rank, in the Sheet and on the roster. Their Discord nickname becomes [Callsign] and the first initial and last name, and they receive the rank role. The person must already be in the Discord server.

Recruits in training

To reach the Exit Exam a recruit needs Basic Medical (BM), Basic Communication (EC), Basic Vehicle (BV), 5 days TIG and 7 hours of supervised patrol. Academy staff confirm the patrol by hand; the website cannot know it.
